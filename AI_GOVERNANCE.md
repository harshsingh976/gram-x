# GRAM-X — Responsible AI Governance & Decision Support (Phase 9)

## 1. Principles of Responsible Governance AI

1. **AI as Advisor, Never Governor**: AI models assist administrators by summarizing trends, suggesting categories, and identifying anomalies. All legal, administrative, disciplinary, and financial decisions rest solely with authorized human officials.
2. **Grounding in Verified Source Data**: AI-generated executive summaries must cite actual database metrics (e.g., total received, resolved count, percentage changes). Hallucinated statistics are strictly prohibited.
3. **Mandatory Explainability**: Every priority recommendation, SLA risk score, and anomaly alert displays the exact deterministic signals and contributing factors that generated it.
4. **Human-in-the-Loop Override**: Administrators can override any AI suggestion with 1 click. Overrides are recorded in `audit_logs` for continuous model calibration.
5. **Zero Autonomous Punitive Actions**: AI is strictly prevented from reassigning workers, rejecting citizen complaints, changing user roles, or dismissing appeals autonomously.
